# *Dora Hold'em* (宿命公牌) — Official Rulebook

### I. Overview & Components
* **Components**: 1 standard 52-card poker deck (**no Jokers**), poker chips.
* **Player Count**: 3 to 4 players recommended (supports 2 to 5 players).
* **Dealer Rotation**: The Dealer button rotates clockwise after each round. The **Dealer always acts first**.
* **Orbit (Round) Definition**: Before the Dealer's turn begins, the Orbit count increases by $+1$ (the Dealer's initial turn marks the start of Orbit 1).

---

### II. Setup
1. **Shuffle and Deal**:
   * The Dealer shuffles the deck and places it face-down in the center of the table as the **Draw Pile**.
   * Deal **5 face-down hole cards** to each player (private to each player).
   * Reveal **1 community card face-up** in the center of the table.
2. **Establish the Dora (Bonus Card)**:
   * **The rank of this first revealed community card becomes the designated "Dora" for the entire round** (e.g., if a 7 of Spades is revealed, all 7s in the deck become Dora cards).
   * **Rank Lock**: The Dora rank remains fixed for the entire round, even if this physical card is later swapped or banished.
   * This first community card itself acts as an active, shared Dora card available to all players.

---

### III. Turn Actions (Choose 1 of 5 Each Turn)
On their turn, each player must choose and perform **exactly one** of the following 5 actions:

* **1. Discard & Draw (Hand Cycling)**:
  * Discard up to 2 cards from your hand, then draw an equal number of replacement cards from the draw pile.
  * **Visibility Rule**: 
    * If discarding **2 cards**, exactly **1 must be discarded face-up and 1 face-down**.
    * If discarding **1 card**, it must be discarded **face-up**.
* **2. Manipulate the Board (Tactical Adjustment)**:
  * Choose **one** of the following two options:
    * **[Swap]**: Exchange 1 card from your hand with any 1 card in the community area. The card taken into your hand **must remain face-up** (as an exposed hole card) and **cannot be swapped back into the community area**. If this card is discarded later via Action 1, it **must be discarded face-up**.
    * **[Banish & Refresh]**: Move any 1 community card directly into the discard pile, then reveal the top card of the draw pile to take its place.
* **3. Expand the Board (Raise)**:
  * Reveal the top card of the draw pile and place it face-up directly into the community area. The number of community cards permanently increases by $+1$ (which permanently increases the Base Score by $+1$).
* **4. Fold (Cut Losses)**:
  * Pay chips equal to the **Current Orbit count** into the table pot (e.g., pay 1 chip on Orbit 1, 2 chips on Orbit 2, etc.).
  * Place your hand face-down into the discard pile and exit the round. You take no further actions this round and are **completely exempt from all final showdown loss liabilities**.
* **5. Call Showdown (Resolution)**:
  * Call an immediate showdown. All player turns stop immediately, and the game proceeds directly to hand evaluation.

---

### IV. Game End Conditions
The round ends immediately and proceeds to showdown evaluation when any of the following occurs:
1. **Voluntary Showdown**: A player executes **Action 5: Call Showdown**.
2. **Deck Depletion (Sudden Death)**: At the start of any player's turn, if the draw pile has **fewer than 2 cards**, the game **ends immediately**, and all remaining active players enter a forced showdown (the discard pile is never reshuffled).
3. **Lone Survivor**: If all other players fold, leaving only 1 active player, that player **wins immediately** and sweeps all chips in the pot (all fold fees).

---

### V. Showdown & Hand Rankings
1. **Hand Construction**: Every active (non-folded) player selects the **best 5-card poker hand** possible using any combination of their 5 hole cards and all community cards.
2. **Hand Hierarchy**: Follows standard Texas Hold'em rankings:
   $$\text{Royal Flush} > \text{Straight Flush} > \text{Four of a Kind} > \text{Full House} > \text{Flush} > \text{Straight} > \text{Three of a Kind} > \text{Two Pair} > \text{One Pair} > \text{High Card}$$
   *Suits have no ranking; identical hands result in a tie (Chop).*
3. **Roles**: The player with the highest hand is the **Winner**; all other active players are **Active Losers**.

---

### VI. Scoring & Settlement

#### 1. Base Score Formula
$$\text{Base Score} = \text{Final Community Card Count} + \text{Final Orbit Number} + \text{Dora Cards in Winner's Hand}$$
* *Note: Each Dora card adds +1 point to the Base Score, and applies **only if the Dora card is included in the Winner's best 5-card winning hand**.*

#### 2. Payout Rules (Fixed Loss, Winner Takes All)
* **Active Losers**: Each active loser pays **exactly 1x Base Score** directly to the winner.
* **Folded Players**: Fold fees paid earlier remain in the pot.
* **Winner's Reward**: The winner sweeps **all chips on the table** (all Base Score payments from active losers $+$ all fold fees; does not include the Dealer's orbit tracking chips).

#### 3. Ties and Odd Chip Rules (Chop)
* If multiple players tie for the best hand, all chips in the pot are **split equally** among the tied winners.
* **Odd Chip Priority**: If the pot cannot be divided evenly, any remainder chips are awarded to the tied winner **closest to the Dealer's left** (starting with the Dealer position, moving clockwise).

---

### VII. Example Walkthrough

* **Scenario**: 4-player game (A is Dealer; B, C, and D are non-dealers).
  * The first community card revealed is the 9 of Diamonds (**9 is locked as the Dora rank**).
  * **Orbit 1**: C has a terrible hand, chooses Action 4 to **Fold**, and pays **1 chip** into the pot.
  * **Orbit 2**: There are now 3 community cards on the board. A feels confident and uses Action 5 to **Call Showdown**!
* **Showdown**:
  * **A (Dealer)**: Wins with Three of a Kind containing one Dora 9.
  * **B & D**: Weaker hands; both are Active Losers.
* **Settlement**:
  1. **Base Score** = 3 (Community Cards) $+$ 2 (Orbit) $+$ 1 (A's Dora 9) = **6 points**.
  2. **Active Loser Payouts**: B pays 6 points, D pays 6 points.
  3. **A's Total Winnings**: Collects B (6) $+$ D (6) $+$ C's fold fee (1) = **13 points**.

---

### VIII. Table Etiquette & FAQ

#### 1. Physical Card Placement (Preventing Confusion):
* **[Face-Down Discards]**: Placed face-down into the shared central discard pile.
* **[Face-Up Discards]**: Placed face-up and **splayed horizontally in a cascade** in front of the player (leaving corner indices visible to save table space while allowing all players to inspect dead cards).
* **[Swapped Exposed Cards]**: Any community card acquired via "Swap" is placed face-up, **oriented vertically side-by-side (unsplayed/unstacked)** in front of the player, clearly indicating it as an exposed, active hole card.

#### 2. Visual Orbit Tracking:
* The Dealer tracks the current orbit using poker chips. Before acting in Orbit 1, the Dealer places 1 chip in front of themselves; at the start of each subsequent orbit, the Dealer stacks 1 additional chip on top. The height of this chip stack serves as the official indicator for the current Orbit number at all times.

#### 3. Dora Eligibility (Cards Speak / Objective Best Hand Principle):
* At showdown, a player's 5-card hand is **strictly determined by the mathematically highest objective poker hand combination**; players may not voluntarily downgrade kickers to claim Dora points.
* *Example*: If a player's absolute best 5-card hand is **[A-A-Q-10-9]** (Pair of Aces with a 9 kicker), and the community card has a Dora 2 where **[A-A-Q-10-2]** would still beat all opponents, the player **cannot** swap the 9 for the 2 to gain $+1$ Dora point. A Dora point is awarded only if the Dora card is naturally part of that player's true, objective best 5-card combination.
